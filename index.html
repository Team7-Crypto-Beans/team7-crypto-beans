HOW IT ALL BEGAN

One Bean.
A Whole Universe.

Team 7 Crypto Beans began with a simple idea: create a character and build a universe around it together. At the center stands Jerry Beans Man — crowned, armored, and ready to guard the first golden portal.

But this is bigger than one character. Seven portals mark the beginning of a growing collection of Beans Men, each with a place in the universe and a story still to be written.
